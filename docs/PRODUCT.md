# AURA AI OS

## Vision

AURA is an AI Operating System designed to become the most intelligent personal AI assistant capable of understanding voice, vision, memory, and real-world actions.

Unlike traditional chatbots, AURA is designed to think, remember, plan, and perform tasks across Windows, Android, Web, and Cloud.

---

# Mission

Build an AI assistant that helps people accomplish real work instead of only answering questions.
